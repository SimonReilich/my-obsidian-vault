# Identitäten
- $\begin{pmatrix} n + k - 1 \\ k - 1 \end{pmatrix} = |\{(x_1, ..., x_k) \in \mathbb{N}^k \mid x_1 + ... + x_k = n\}|$ 
- $\sum_{k = 0}^n \begin{pmatrix} n \\ k \end{pmatrix} = 2^n$ 
- $\sum_{i = k}^n \begin{pmatrix} i \\ k \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} n + 1 \\ k + 1 \end{pmatrix}$ 
- $\sum_{k = 0}^n k * \begin{pmatrix} n \\ k \end{pmatrix} = n * 2^{n - 1}$ 
- $\sum_{k = 0}^n \begin{pmatrix} n \\ k \end{pmatrix}^2 = \begin{pmatrix} 2n \\ n \end{pmatrix}$
