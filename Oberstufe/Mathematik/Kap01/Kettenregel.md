#Oberstufe #Mathematik 
# Satz
$$(f(g(x)))' = f'(g(x)) * g'(x)$$
# Beweis
$(f(g(x_0)))' = \lim_{x \to x_0} \frac{f(g(x)) - f(g(x_0))}{x - x_0} = \lim_{x \to x_0} \frac{f(g(x)) - f(g(x_0))}{g(x) - g(x_0)} * \frac{g(x) - g(x_0)}{x - x_0} = f'(g(x_0)) * g'(x_0)$ q.e.d.
